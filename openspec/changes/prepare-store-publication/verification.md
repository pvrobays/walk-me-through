## Group 2 — Icons (2026-10-22)

### 2.1 Generator

- `scripts/build-icons.mjs` (`npm run icons:build`) draws one geometry in a 24-unit grid: a rounded tile, the first step as an outline, a connector, and the current step filled. It writes `packages/vscode/media/icon.png` (256×256 RGBA, 4,984 bytes), `pluginIcon.svg` (40×40, tile `#3574F0`) and `pluginIcon_dark.svg` (40×40, tile `#548AF7`), both with a white mark.
- The PNG uses 4×4 supersampling and a small fixed-Huffman deflate encoder in the script instead of `node:zlib`, so the bytes do not depend on the zlib build or CPU features of the machine.
- `npm test` runs `npm run check:icons`, which regenerates the icons in memory and compares them byte for byte. Deliberately changing one byte of the PNG, and one colour in `pluginIcon_dark.svg`, made the check fail (exit 1) naming the file; restoring them made it pass.
- `packages/vscode/unit/icons.test.ts` checks the PNG signature, chunk CRCs, a 256×256 8-bit RGBA header, and that the inflated pixels equal the rasterized mark; it also checks that both SVGs declare 40×40. `System.Drawing` decodes the PNG as 256×256 `Format32bppArgb`.

### 2.2 Owner review

The owner reviewed the icons on light and dark backgrounds, with the PNG at 128 and 64 px and the SVGs at 80 and 40 px, and **approved them as is** (2026-10-22). The owner also accepted the two departures from the original D2 text: the arrow chevron is dropped in favour of a filled current step, and the PNG uses the script's own deflate encoder. `design.md` D2 is updated to match.
